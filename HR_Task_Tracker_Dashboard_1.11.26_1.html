How to Use This Tracker

STEP 1Update team members, Assigned-By names and Job Functions in the Lists tab. Use + Add to add more entries to any list.
STEP 2Click + New Task (top-right, or Alt+N) and fill in the form. Click any row in Task Tracker to edit it; ✓ marks it complete; 🗑 deletes it. Use Sheet View for Excel-style bulk editing. The 24 sample rows are illustrative. Clear them from the Lists tab, or overwrite them.
STEP 3Open Dashboard and use the yellow dropdowns (Priority / Assigned By / Team Member / Job Function) to filter. Summary follows the same filters.
SAVINGData saves automatically in this browser on this computer. Use Backup (JSON) regularly and Restore Backup to move data to another computer or browser. Export CSV opens in Excel.

BUSINESS RULES
Due DateSame as Tentative Date to Complete.
Remaining DaysTentative Date to Complete − Report Date. Negative (red) = days overdue; 0–3 amber; “Done” when completed.
CompletedA task is Completed when a Completion Date is entered OR Completion Percentage = 100%.
OverdueNot completed and the Report Date is past the Due Date.
Not Started / In ProgressNot overdue and Completion % = 0 → Not Started; otherwise In Progress.
On-Time DeliveryCompleted tasks whose Completion Date ≤ Due Date, divided by completed tasks that have a Completion Date.
Job FunctionType of work the task belongs to (e.g. HR Analytics, Recruitment). Drives the Job Function chart and summary table.
Team workloadA task counts once for each person named in Assigned To 1, 2 or 3.
Trend chartRolling 12 months ending in the Report Date month.
